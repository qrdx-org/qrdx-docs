---
title: Quickstart
description: Install QRDX Wallet, connect it to testnet, and place your first order on the native exchange.
icon: Rocket
---

This takes about five minutes. Everything happens on **testnet**, where tokens have no value.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Install QRDX Wallet

Pick the platform you will trade from. All three share the same accounts, and one recovery phrase restores them anywhere.

| Platform | How |
| --- | --- |
| Browser extension (Chrome, Firefox) | Install from [wallet.qrdx.org](https://wallet.qrdx.org). Sites find it automatically. |
| iPhone | Open [wallet.qrdx.org](https://wallet.qrdx.org) in Safari, then **Share → Add to Home Screen**. Unlocks with Face ID on iOS 18 and later. |
| Any browser | Use the web app at [wallet.qrdx.org](https://wallet.qrdx.org/wallet). It locks when you reload. |

</Step>
<Step>

### Create a wallet and write down the phrase

Choose **Create a new wallet**, set a password, and write down the 12-word recovery phrase. The phrase is the only backup: it restores both of your keys, the classic one and the post-quantum one.

Each account has two addresses:

- a **`0x…` address** for classic (Ethereum-style) transactions, and
- a **`0xPQ…` address**, signed with your post-quantum key. Trading, tokens and pools use this one.

[Accounts](/docs/concepts/accounts) explains how the two relate.

</Step>
<Step>

### Switch to testnet

Open the network menu in the wallet and choose **QRDX Testnet**. The trading site follows the wallet's network.

</Step>
<Step>

### Get testnet QRDX

Exchange operations pay a small fee in QRDX (about 0.0001 QRDX each). There is no public testnet faucet yet; ask in the QRDX community channels for testnet QRDX, and give your `0xPQ…` address.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect to QRDX Trade and place an order

Open [trade.qrdx.org](https://trade.qrdx.org), choose **Connect**, and approve in the wallet. With the extension installed, pick **QRDX Wallet extension**; on a phone, pick **QRDX Wallet on your phone** and scan the QR code.

Pick a market, enter a price and size, and press **Buy** or **Sell**. The wallet shows the decoded order ("Buy 10 USDC at 0.0043 WQRDX") before you sign. The order executes when the next block includes it, usually within seconds.

</Step>
<Step>

### See it on the explorer

Every order, fill and fee is on [explorer.qrdx.org](https://explorer.qrdx.org). Search for your `0xPQ…` address; you can also [claim a public profile](/docs/guides/explorer#claim-your-address) for it.

</Step>
</Steps>
