Every pair of native tokens, and any token against native QRDX, can trade in two venues at once: an **order book** and one or more **pools**. Both are part of the chain, so a trade is final when a block includes it.

## Markets

[trade.qrdx.org](https://trade.qrdx.org) lists every market with a pool. A market's URL names it: verified assets by name (`/trade/btc/usdc`), anything else by token address. Anyone can create a token with any name, so the site marks unverified tokens and the wallet always shows a token's address next to its symbol.

Native QRDX trades directly; there is no wrapping. Its side of a trade settles in your QRDX balance.

## Limit orders

A limit order rests on the book until it fills or you cancel it. While it rests, its funds are escrowed: a buy holds the quote amount (price × size), a sell holds the base amount. Cancelling refunds the escrow.

1. Pick a market and the **Limit** tab.
2. Enter a price and a size; the form shows the total and your available balance.
3. Press **Buy** or **Sell** and approve in the wallet.

The order appears in **Open orders** once a block includes it. Several orders can be placed before the first is included: the wallet tracks the nonces it has submitted.

### Managing open orders

- **Edit** (the pencil) changes an order's price or size. On a network with the 2026-10 trading features it changes in one step (`MODIFY_ORDER`), and a smaller size at the same price keeps its place in the queue. On an older network the order is replaced: cancelled, then placed again at the new terms, one signature each; the new order joins the back of the queue, and if the cancel lands but the new order is refused, no order is left.
- **Cancel all** cancels every open order on the list. With the 2026-10 features it is one approval and one transaction, all or nothing; before them each cancel is its own transaction, so the wallet asks for each.

### Stops, time in force and hidden orders

Where the network runs the 2026-10 trading features:

- The **Stop** tab places a stop-loss or take-profit that waits for the pair's **last trade** to reach its trigger, then trades as a market order (at most 10 % through the trigger) or a limit at the price you give. Its funds are locked when you place it and come back if you cancel it (the **Stops** tab).
- Limit orders take a **time in force** (GTC, Post only, IOC, FOK), and can be **hidden** or an **iceberg**.
- A buy can carry a **TP / SL**: one sell trigger that holds exactly what the buy bought, the first of the two to fire cancelling the other.

The same works for perps orders.

## Swaps and market orders

A swap spends a fixed amount and receives whatever the best venue pays:

- each of the pair's **pools**, and
- the **book**, walking the bids when you sell the base asset or the asks when you buy it.

The swap settles entirely with the venue that pays the most. A pair with no market of its own is routed through up to three others; on a network with the 2026-10 features that is one swap through the whole path, delivering at least the minimum or nothing. Before you sign, the site asks the node for an exact quote and sets **minimum received** to the quote less your slippage tolerance (0.5 % by default). If the price moves past that before the block, the swap fails and nothing changes, except the small transaction fee.

<Callout type="info" title="Every operation is all-or-nothing">
  A swap that would receive less than its minimum, an order you cannot afford, or a cancel of someone else's order is refused whole. The chain records the failure; your balances do not change except for the fee.
</Callout>

## Alerts

The bell next to a market's name sets a **price alert**: above or below a price in the market's quote asset (USD for perps). The site checks it against the last price while a QRDX Trade tab is open and tells you once, with a browser notification if you allow them, and then removes it. You are also told when one of your resting orders fills.

## Fees

- **Pool fees** depend on the pool's tier: 0.01 %, 0.05 %, 0.3 % or 1 %. 70 % go to the liquidity providers in range, 30 % to the protocol.
- **Transaction fees** are paid in QRDX and burned: about 0.00004–0.00015 QRDX per operation, whether it succeeds or fails. On networks with the 2026-10 release, trading operations (orders, cancels, edits, stops, swaps) go free while your account has free actions left (10,000 to start, more as you trade; it needs 0.025 QRDX or 10 of perps equity to use them).
- **Book fees** follow your 14-day perps volume: 0.05 % taker and 0.02 % maker at first, lower from 5M.

## Charts and data

Prices, candles, trades and 24-hour statistics come from the chain's own fills and pool swaps. The order book's **Depth** view draws the book as cumulative size against price; click it to use a price in the order form. Where a token has an outside market, the site also shows its USD **index price** from public exchanges, labelled as reference data. Nothing is generated or padded.
