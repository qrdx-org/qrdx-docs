---
title: Reporting a vulnerability
description: How to tell us about a security problem.
icon: Bug
---

Email **security@qrdx.org** with what you found, how to reproduce it, and its impact. Please do not open a public issue or post about it until it is fixed.

Include, if you can:

- which component (node, wallet, trade, explorer, relay) and version or commit,
- the network (testnet, or a local node),
- steps or a proof of concept,
- what an attacker could do with it.

We will acknowledge your report, keep you updated while we fix it, and credit you if you wish.

**Testnet first.** Test against testnet or your own node, never against other people's accounts or funds, and do not degrade the service for others.
