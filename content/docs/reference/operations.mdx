---
title: Exchange operations
description: Every operation an exchange transaction can carry, its parameters, who may send it, and its gas.
icon: ListOrdered
---

An exchange transaction's `op_type` is the number or the name. Amounts, sizes and prices are decimal strings with at most 18 decimal places. Every operation is all-or-nothing: one that cannot execute is refused whole, changes nothing, and still pays its gas. [Exchange transactions](/docs/build/exchange-transactions) explains the envelope and signing.

**Fee** = gas × `gas_price` (at least 1 gwei = 10⁻⁹ QRDX per gas), paid in QRDX and burned. At the minimum price, 40,000 gas costs 0.00004 QRDX.

## Spot

| Op | # | Params | Gas |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `CREATE_POOL` | 1 | `token0`, `token1`, `fee_tier` (100 \| 500 \| 3000 \| 10000), `pool_type` (`STANDARD` \| `SUBSIDIZED`), `initial_price` (token1 per token0 of the sorted pair), `stake_amount` (QRDX) | 150,000 |
| `ADD_LIQUIDITY` | 2 | `pool_id` (or `token0` + `token1` [+ `fee_tier`]), `tick_lower`, `tick_upper` (multiples of the tick spacing), `amount` (the liquidity L) | 90,000 |
| `REMOVE_LIQUIDITY` | 3 | `pool_id`, `position_id`, `amount?` (L; default all, `0` collects fees only). Owner only | 60,000 |
| `SWAP` | 4 | `token_in`, `token_out`, `amount_in`, `min_amount_out`, `deadline?` (block time), `venue?` (`auto` \| `amm` \| `clob`), `pool_id?` | 65,000 |
| `PLACE_ORDER` | 5 | `pair` (`tokenA:tokenB`, either order), `side` (`buy` \| `sell`), `order_type` (`limit`), `price`, `amount` (base) | 40,000 |
| `CANCEL_ORDER` | 6 | `order_id`, `pair` | 25,000 |
| `REMOVE_POOL` | 17 | `pool_id`. The creator, once no position remains; refunds the stake | 60,000 |

Native QRDX is named `QRDX` (any casing) in place of a token address.

## Perpetuals

| Op | # | Params | Gas |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `CREATE_MARKET` | 12 | `base_token` (e.g. `BTC`), `max_leverage?` (default 20) | 100,000 |

| `PERP_WITHDRAW` | 19 | `amount`: at most `withdrawable` | 40,000 |
| `PERP_SET_LEVERAGE` | 20 | `market_id`, `leverage`, `mode` (`cross` \| `isolated`) | 20,000 |
| `PERP_ORDER` | 21 | `market_id`, `side`, `size`, `price`, `reduce_only?`, `tif?` (`gtc` default \| `ioc`) | 60,000 |
| `PERP_CANCEL` | 22 | `market_id`, `order_id` | 25,000 |
| `VAULT_DEPOSIT` | 23 | `amount`: free collateral into the backstop vault, for shares | 40,000 |

An order's id is the first 16 characters of its transaction hash; the receipt returns it too.

## Retired

Operations 7–10 (`OPEN_POSITION`, `CLOSE_POSITION`, `PARTIAL_CLOSE`, `ADD_MARGIN`) belonged to an earlier perpetuals engine and are refused; use `PERP_*`. Operation 11, `UPDATE_ORACLE`, sets an oracle price from a configured reporter on development networks only; public networks configure none, and prices come from validators' `ORACLE_VOTE`s.
